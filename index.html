Ugly Notetaker

Ugly Notetaker is a personal, single-user voice capture and memory tool. An Android phone records spoken moments during hours its owner sets, uploads them to a private server that the owner runs, and turns them into transcripts and notes that only the owner can search.

It is not a commercial product and has no users other than its owner.

Google account access is used for two things only: storing encrypted backups in the owner's own Google Drive, and reading the owner's own calendar to label conversations with the meeting they belong to.
